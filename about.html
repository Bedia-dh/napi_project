About NAPI

Shaping Policy Through Evidence & Youth Leadership

The North Africa Policy Institute bridges research, advocacy, and youth engagement to build more equitable societies across the Maghreb region and beyond.

Our Mission

To produce rigorous, independent policy research and empower young advocates to translate evidence into meaningful change across North Africa and the broader Arab world.

Our Vision

A region where evidence-based policy, inclusive institutions, and a vibrant generation of civic leaders drive sustainable human development and regional cooperation.

What Drives Us
Our Core Values

Evidence-Based Rigor

Every recommendation is grounded in robust data, peer-reviewed methods, and transparent analysis.

Youth Empowerment

Young people are not just beneficiaries — they are co-creators of the research and advocacy we produce.

Regional Solidarity

We believe cross-border collaboration is essential to addressing shared challenges in the Maghreb.

Independence

Our analysis is free from political or donor influence — we follow the evidence wherever it leads.

Social Justice

Equity, inclusion, and the rights of marginalized communities sit at the heart of every project we undertake.

Innovation

We experiment with new methods — from participatory research to digital advocacy — to maximize our impact.

Our Story

From a Conversation to a Movement

NAPI was founded in 2017 by a small group of researchers and civil society leaders who saw a critical gap: North Africa produced world-class scholars, yet the region lacked a dedicated institution to translate that knowledge into actionable policy recommendations.

What began as informal roundtables in Tunis quickly grew into a structured institute with flagship programs, international partnerships, and a network of over 200 young policy leaders across 12 countries.

Today NAPI is recognized as one of the leading independent think tanks in the Arab world, producing research on health equity, climate, governance, and youth participation — always with young voices at the table.
