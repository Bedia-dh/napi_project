Our Programs
Flagship Initiatives
Four programs that bring research to life through youth leadership
Youth Policy Lab

YPL — Shaping Future Policy Leaders

A year-long fellowship pairing 25 young researchers with senior mentors to produce policy papers on North Africa's most pressing challenges.

Explore YPL
Chill Chat

Chill Chat — Conversations That Matter

A regular online dialogue series bringing together young professionals across borders to discuss policy, identity, and social change.

Explore Chill Chat
Policy Labs

Policy Labs — From Idea to Advocacy

Intensive co-design workshops where young advocates develop and pitch policy solutions to government stakeholders and civil society.

Explore Policy Labs
Youth Voices

Youth Voices — Publishing Young Perspectives

An open platform for young writers across North Africa to publish opinion pieces, field reports, and advocacy blogs on issues they care about.
